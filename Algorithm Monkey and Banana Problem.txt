Algorithm: Monkey and Banana Problem

START

1. Initialize:
      Monkey = Door
      Box = Window
      Banana = Center

2. Move the monkey to the box.

3. Push the box to the position under the banana.

4. Move the monkey along with the box.

5. Climb onto the box.

6. Pick the banana.

7. Display "Goal Achieved: Monkey has the banana."

STOP
