Algorithm: Crypt Arithmetic Problem

1. Start.
2. Read the given words (SEND + MORE = MONEY).
3. Assign a unique digit (0–9) to each letter.
4. Ensure that the leading letters are not assigned zero.
5. Convert each word into its numeric value.
6. Check whether:
      SEND + MORE = MONEY
7. If the equation is true:
      a. Print the digit assignments.
      b. Print the solution.
8. Otherwise, try another digit assignment.
9. Repeat until a valid solution is found.
10. Stop.
